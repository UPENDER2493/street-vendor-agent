# UPI & Digital Payment Setup Guide for Street Vendors

## Why digital payments matter for a vendor

- No cash-handling risk; no disputes over change.
- Automatic transaction record — useful for loan applications (PM SVANidhi tranche 2/3 requires
  digital payment history for cashback and credibility).
- PM SVANidhi cashback: vendors with 50+ digital transactions/month can earn up to ₹100/month.
- Customers — especially younger, office-going demographics — increasingly prefer paying by phone.
- Digital payment history builds a credit footprint useful for future formal credit access.

## What is UPI?

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is a real-time payment system built by NPCI (National Payments
Corporation of India) that allows instant money transfer between bank accounts using a mobile
number or UPI ID. It is free for individuals and most small merchants.

## What you need before starting UPI setup

1. A savings bank account (Jan Dhan account is also accepted).
2. A mobile number registered with the bank account.
3. A smartphone with internet access.

## Step-by-step UPI setup for a vendor (general guidance — exact screens vary by app)

**Step 1:** Install a UPI app — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, or your bank's own mobile app.
All are free to download from the Play Store (Android) or App Store (iPhone).

**Step 2:** Open the app → "Create Account" or "Register" → enter your mobile number → verify
via OTP (One-Time Password sent by SMS to your registered mobile number).

**Step 3:** Link your bank account — the app will detect accounts linked to your mobile number.
Select the correct account. Set a UPI PIN using your debit card details (last 6 digits + expiry)
or Aadhaar-based OTP (on some apps).

**Step 4:** Get your QR code for the stall — inside the app, look for "My QR Code" / "Merchant
QR" / "Business QR". This generates a unique QR code customers can scan to pay you directly.

**Step 5:** Print/display the QR code — download and get it printed (laminated card or sticker).
Place it prominently on your cart or stall. Many print shops offer this at low cost; some UPI apps
(PhonePe Business, Google Pay for Business) offer free printed QR sticker delivery.

## Setting up a business UPI account (recommended once volume grows)

Apps like **PhonePe Business**, **Google Pay for Business**, and **Paytm for Business** offer:
- A separate business profile with shop name
- Daily collection tracking and settlement history
- Basic invoicing/billing features
- GST invoice generation (for those registered)

These are free to set up and useful for accounting and future loan applications.

## Common problems and quick fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| OTP not received | Ensure mobile number matches the bank's registered number; check signal/data |
| Payment failed but money deducted | Usually auto-reverses in 1–3 business days; don't collect double payment |
| Forgot UPI PIN | Reset in-app using debit card number, expiry, and OTP |
| QR code not scanning | Ensure QR is not crumpled/faded; regenerate and reprint |
| App says "bank account not found" | Visit bank branch to link/update mobile number |

## Best practices

- **Verify before handing goods:** Check payment confirmation sound or screen notification —
  a customer's screenshot of "payment sent" is NOT proof; wait for the confirmation on YOUR phone.
- **Keep a backup:** On network outage days, keep a small "UPI down — cash accepted" sign ready.
- **Track daily UPI collections:** Even a rough daily tally helps when applying for tranche 2 or 3
  of PM SVANidhi or future Mudra/bank loans.
- **Do NOT share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank password with anyone** — no legitimate bank, app,
  or government official will ever ask for these.

## QR code guidance

A QR code for payment is NOT a UPI ID/PIN — it is just a scannable link to your payment address.
It is safe to display publicly. Customers scan it and enter the amount they want to pay.

For permanent display, use static QR codes (generated from your UPI app's "My QR Code" section).
For variable amounts, dynamic QR codes are generated per transaction (available in business apps).
