# Digital Onboarding for Street Vendors

> A step-by-step guide for vendors who are new to digital tools. All tools mentioned here are
> free. The agent must NEVER imply these steps are completed automatically — the vendor must
> perform them directly.

## Recommended digital onboarding sequence

1. **Bank account + mobile number linked** — foundation for everything else.
2. **UPI setup** — start accepting digital payments immediately.
3. **WhatsApp Business** — professional customer communication.
4. **Google Business Profile** — get discovered on Google Maps and Search.
5. **e-Shram registration** — government identity + insurance for unorganised workers.
6. **PM SVANidhi** — working capital loan once above steps are done.

---

## WhatsApp Business Setup (free, ~10 minutes)

WhatsApp Business is a free version of WhatsApp designed for small businesses with professional
features not available in regular WhatsApp.

**What you need:** Smartphone with internet + a phone number (can be same as regular WhatsApp —
chat history migrates if you choose).

**Setup steps:**
1. Download "WhatsApp Business" from Play Store (Android) or App Store (iPhone) — free.
2. Open app → agree to terms → verify phone number via OTP.
3. Go to Settings → Business Profile → fill in:
   - Business name
   - Category (Grocery / Food & Drink / Shopping / etc.)
   - Description (2–3 sentences: what you sell, where, hours)
   - Address (locality + city)
   - Business hours
   - Profile photo (clear photo of stall or products)
4. Set up Quick Replies (Settings → Business Tools → Quick Replies) for common questions.
5. Set a Greeting Message for new customers (auto-sent on first contact).
6. Add a Product Catalog (Settings → Catalog → Add Product) with photos and prices.

**Sharing your WhatsApp Business link:** Your link is wa.me/91[your10digitnumber] (e.g.,
wa.me/919876543210). Share this on your Google Business Profile, stall signage, and with customers.

**Broadcast lists:** Message up to 256 saved contacts at once. They receive it as a personal
message (not a group chat). Use for weekly offers and new stock updates.

## Google Business Profile Setup (free, ~20–30 minutes)

**What you need:** Any Google/Gmail account.

**Setup steps:**
1. Visit business.google.com → sign in → "Add your business."
2. Enter business name → select best matching category.
3. Add location — drag pin precisely to stall location on map.
4. Add phone number and business hours.
5. Add 3–5 photos (stall, products, area).
6. Write a 2–3 sentence business description (see local_seo.md for template).
7. Complete verification — Google may verify via postcard (5–7 days), phone call, or video.

**After setup:** Reply to all reviews. Post updates ("Add Update/Offer") for new stock and offers.

**IMPORTANT for the agent:** The agent generates profile content for the vendor to copy-paste.
The agent does NOT create or submit the Google listing directly. The vendor must do this themselves.

## e-Shram Registration (free, ~15 minutes)

**What you need:** Aadhaar number + Aadhaar-linked mobile number.

**Setup steps:**
1. Visit eshram.gov.in → "Register on e-Shram" → enter Aadhaar number.
2. Enter OTP sent to Aadhaar-linked mobile.
3. Fill in: name, date of birth, address, occupation (select "Street Vendor" from dropdown).
4. Add bank account details (for future benefit transfers).
5. Download e-Shram card (PDF) — contains 12-digit UAN number.
6. Alternatively: visit any Common Service Centre (CSC) for free assisted registration.

**Benefits:**
- Official identity proof for unorganised workers
- Linked to PM Suraksha Bima Yojana: ₹2 lakh accidental insurance at ₹12/year
- Facilitates access to government welfare schemes
- Required/helpful for PM SVANidhi and other vendor benefit schemes

## Basic digital skills for first-time smartphone users

- **Installing an app:** Open Play Store → search app name → Install → Open.
- **OTP:** A 4–6 digit One-Time Password sent by SMS. Enter within 2–3 minutes. NEVER share with
  anyone. No legitimate bank, app, or government portal will call and ask for your OTP.
- **Strong signal needed:** UPI, OTP, and app installs require good mobile data or WiFi.
- **Photos for profile:** Bright daylight photos without backlighting work best for stall and
  product photos used in Google Business Profile and WhatsApp Catalog.

---

## Agent guidance principles

- All listed tools are free. Reassure vendors that no payment is required to set up.
- Explain acronyms: UPI = Unified Payments Interface, OTP = One Time Password, UAN = Universal
  Account Number.
- Sequence matters: UPI before SVANidhi (required for digital cashback eligibility).
- The agent provides guidance only — it cannot complete registrations on the vendor's behalf.
