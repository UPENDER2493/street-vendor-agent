# Government & MSME Schemes for Street Vendors

> IMPORTANT: Figures below are indicative and may be revised. Always direct the vendor to confirm
> current amounts, eligibility, and deadlines on the official portal or with their bank/ULB.
> This document should NOT be used as a substitute for official scheme guidelines.

## Scheme: PM SVANidhi (Pradhan Mantri Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi)

**What it is:** A central government scheme launched in June 2020, specifically for urban street
vendors, giving collateral-free working capital loans with no guarantor needed. Extended as
"PM SVANidhi 2.0" through March 2030, with a RuPay credit card facility for vendors who repay well.

**Who is eligible:** Any street vendor selling goods or services in streets, markets, or public
spaces — food vendors, fruit/vegetable sellers, tea stalls, roadside clothing and footwear sellers,
artisans, book/stationery sellers, etc. You need a **Certificate of Vending (CoV)** issued by your
Urban Local Body (ULB/municipal corporation). If you don't have one, a **Letter of Recommendation
(LoR)** from the ULB can be used to apply.

**Loan structure (progressive tranches based on timely repayment):**
- 1st loan: ₹10,000–₹15,000 (working capital, 1 year tenure; exact amount varies by ULB cycle)
- 2nd loan: ₹20,000 (after full, on-time repayment of the 1st loan)
- 3rd loan: ₹50,000 (after full, on-time repayment of the 2nd loan)
- No collateral, no guarantor, no processing fee.

**Interest subsidy:** 7% per annum interest subsidy, credited quarterly directly into the loan
account. This effectively brings the cost close to zero for on-time repayers.

**Digital payment cashback:** Vendors who use UPI/digital payments regularly (approx. 50+
transactions/month in most cycles) can earn up to ₹100/month cashback (up to ₹1,200/year).

**How to apply (as per official portal guidance):**
1. Visit: pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in OR apply at the Urban Local Body (municipal office) directly.
2. Verify mobile number via OTP; obtain SRN (Survey Reference Number) if not already allotted.
3. Fill loan application with personal, business, and bank details; attach self-attested KYC
   documents (Aadhaar, vendor certificate/LoR, bank passbook copy).
4. ULB verifies vendor status; bank/NBFC/MFI conducts basic KYC and credit check.
5. On approval, sign the loan agreement — funds typically credited within 1–2 weeks.
6. Helpline: 1800-11-1979 (toll-free, multiple Indian languages).

## Scheme: PM Mudra Yojana (MUDRA Loans)

**Source:** Ministry of Finance / MUDRA (Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency)
**URL:** https://www.mudra.org.in

**What it is:** Collateral-free business loans for non-farm income-generating micro and small
enterprises. Three loan categories:
- **Shishu:** Up to ₹50,000
- **Kishor:** ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh
- **Tarun:** ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh

**Who is eligible:** Non-farm small businesses, micro-entrepreneurs, vendors with growth plans.
Available from public/private banks, MFIs, NBFCs, cooperative banks, RRBs.

**How to apply:** Apply at any participating bank branch or through the Udyami Mitra portal
(https://www.udyamimitra.in).

## Scheme: PM Vishwakarma Yojana

**Source:** Ministry of MSME
**URL:** https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in

**What it is:** A scheme for traditional artisans and craftspeople — tailors, cobblers, carpenters,
potters, weavers, blacksmiths, goldsmiths, etc. Provides:
- Skill training (15 days basic + advanced training)
- Toolkit support (₹15,000 toolkit voucher)
- Collateral-free credit (₹1 lakh first tranche, ₹2 lakh second tranche at concessional interest)
- Digital transaction incentive (₹1/transaction, up to ₹100/month)
- Market linkage support

**Who is eligible:** Traditional artisans practicing 18 specified crafts/trades. Must register
at pmvishwakarma.gov.in.

## Scheme: e-Shram Registration

**Source:** Ministry of Labour & Employment
**URL:** https://eshram.gov.in

**What it is:** National database of unorganised workers. Provides UAN (Universal Account Number)
and identity proof for unorganised sector workers including street vendors.

**Benefits:**
- Free e-Shram card with 12-digit UAN
- Linked to PM Suraksha Bima Yojana: accidental insurance of ₹2 lakh (death/permanent disability)
  and ₹1 lakh (partial disability) at ₹12/year premium
- Facilitates access to other government welfare schemes
- Prerequisite for some state-level vendor welfare programs

**How to register:** eshram.gov.in — requires Aadhaar number and Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
Also available at Common Service Centres (CSC) — free service.

## Scheme: Stand-Up India

**Source:** Ministry of Finance
**URL:** https://www.standupmitra.in

**What it is:** Bank loans of ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore for setting up greenfield enterprises. At
least one SC/ST borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch. Collateral or CGTMSE
guarantee cover required. For manufacturing, services, and trading sectors.

---

## Scheme: Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana

**Source:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare
**URL:** https://pmjay.gov.in

**What it is:** Health insurance coverage up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and
tertiary care hospitalization. Covers BPL families and identified beneficiaries. Many unorganised
sector workers may be eligible. Worth checking eligibility for vendor families.

## Agent guidance principles for scheme information

- ALWAYS state that figures and eligibility criteria should be verified at the official portal or
  with the relevant bank/ULB — scheme parameters are revised by the government.
- NEVER invent loan amounts, deadlines, eligibility criteria, or benefits not present above.
- NEVER claim the agent can apply, approve, or guarantee any scheme.
- When a vendor asks in Hindi/Hinglish, respond in the same language/register.
- For schemes not listed here, say: "I could not verify this scheme from my current knowledge base.
  Please check the official portal or contact your nearest ULB/bank/CSC."
