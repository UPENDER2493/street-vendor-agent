# Local SEO & Digital Visibility for Street Vendors

## What "local SEO" means for a vendor (no jargon)

"Local SEO" simply means: when a nearby customer searches on Google or Google Maps for something
you sell ("fruit stall near me", "best chai Sector 14 Gurugram"), your stall should be easy to
find. This costs nothing and takes 20–30 minutes to set up.

## Core steps for free online discovery

### Step 1: Google Business Profile (most impactful — free, 20–30 min)

Visit business.google.com (sign in with any Google account) and add:
- **Business name:** Exact name as used on the stall (e.g., "Sharma Fresh Fruits")
- **Category:** Most relevant category (e.g., "Fruit and vegetable store", "Street food restaurant",
  "Tea house", "General store")
- **Location pin:** Drop exactly at the stall's location on the map
- **Business hours:** Accurate open/close times
- **Phone number:** WhatsApp Business number preferred
- **3–5 photos:** Clear, well-lit photos of the stall, products, and surroundings
- **Business description:** 2–3 sentences mentioning products, location, and speciality

This lets the stall appear on Google Maps and Search with directions, hours, and photos.

**Verification:** Google usually verifies via postcard (5–7 days), phone call, or video. Follow the
prompted option. Unverified listings have limited visibility.

### Step 2: WhatsApp Business profile (free, 10 min)

WhatsApp Business is a free app with professional business features:
- Business name, category, description, address, hours
- Product catalog with photos and prices
- Quick replies for common questions
- Greeting message for new customers
- Broadcast lists for promotions (up to 256 contacts per list)

A consistent phone number on both Google Business Profile and WhatsApp Business helps customers
find and contact the vendor through either channel.

### Step 3: Write a keyword-rich business description

Effective description structure for a street vendor:
`[Business name] in [specific area/colony], [city] — offering [main products]. [One line on what
makes it special: freshness, variety, years of experience]. Open [hours]. [Optional: UPI accepted,
home delivery available].`

Example:
"Sharma Fresh Fruits in Sector 14, Gurugram — offering fresh seasonal fruits daily. Over 8 years
serving local families and office-goers. Open 8am–8pm. UPI/cash accepted."

### Step 4: Use hyperlocal keywords

Use the specific neighbourhood/sector/colony name, not just the city:
- "Sector 14 fruit stall Gurugram" beats "Gurugram fruit stall" for nearby searches
- "chai near Civil Lines Nagpur" beats "Nagpur chai"
- Include landmark references when relevant: "near XYZ Mall", "opposite ABC School"

### Step 5: Ask for Google reviews

Even 5–10 genuine reviews substantially improve visibility in "near me" searches. A simple verbal
ask after a good sale works well. Respond to every review (positive or negative) — it signals
activity to Google and builds trust with prospective customers.

### Step 6: Post regularly on Google Business Profile

Use "Add Update/Offer" posts to share new stock, festivals, offers. Regularly-updated profiles
rank higher in local search and keep repeat customers engaged.

## What NOT to build initially (for MVP vendor)

- No need for a website — Google Business Profile + WhatsApp Business covers most discovery needs.
- No need for paid ads to start — organic profile + reviews + word of mouth is higher-ROI first.
- No need for a separate domain or hosting costs.

## Important disclaimer for the agent

The agent should NOT claim to:
- Submit or verify a Google Business Profile on behalf of the vendor
- Guarantee listing on any platform
- Guarantee that the vendor will appear in any specific search result

The agent generates content and guidance only. The vendor must create and verify accounts directly.
